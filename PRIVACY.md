# CEsim Studio privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

CEsim Studio does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **Your files stay on your device.** Designs, projects and settings are saved only on your computer, in the files you choose and in the app's local storage.
- **No account, no tracking.** The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
- **Circuit from image** talks only to the AI model that runs on your own computer (the default address is `http://127.0.0.1:11434`). Your pictures are not uploaded to the internet.
- **Share links** put the design inside the link itself. Nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to send the link to someone.
- **Downloads.** When you ask the app to download the local AI model, it is fetched from the model provider by the AI program on your computer.

If you have a question, open an issue at https://github.com/iyousef-hub/CEsim-Studio/issues.
